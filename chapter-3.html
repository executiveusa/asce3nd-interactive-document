CHAPTER 03

Month 1 Social System

This chapter turns the strategy into an operating rhythm ASC3ND can actually use: what to publish, when to publish it, how the grid should feel, and how to keep the system going after handoff.

CHAPTER 3 · WEEKLY RHYTHM

Three posts a week. One clear story.

Monday explains who ASC3ND is. Wednesday lets people hear and feel the story through real video. Friday turns that trust into community action.

MONDAY · IDENTITY

Who we are

Mission, values, youth, leadership, and the reason ASC3ND exists.

WEDNESDAY · STORY

Let people feel it

Real voices, real footage, documentary moments, and human story.

FRIDAY · COMMUNITY

Give them somewhere to go

Attend, share, support, volunteer, partner, or see proof of the work.

CHAPTER 3 · VISUAL RHYTHM

Black gives authority. Cream gives breathing room.

The Month 1 feed should feel intentional before someone reads a single caption. Use a disciplined black-and-cream rhythm with gold as a restrained accent—not a rainbow of campaign colors.

BLACK

Authority

Use for strong statements, community proof, event emphasis, and decisive calls to action.

CREAM

Humanity

Use for identity, education, quotes, quieter storytelling, and space around real photography.

CHAPTER 3 · WEDNESDAY REELS

The human center of the week.

These are not polished commercials. They should feel like ASC3ND: real people, real voices, real environments, and one clear idea per Reel.

01

Why We Started

Founding motivation, personal stakes, and the problem ASC3ND exists to address.

02

What a Mentor Can Do

Show mentorship through lived experience, not generic claims.

03

Getting Ready for Community Cuts

Preparation, people, logistics, and anticipation using real footage.

04

Community in Action

After the event, show what happened and what the community made possible.

CHAPTER 3 · HANDOFF

The goal is not dependence. It is a repeatable system.

FIVE OPERATING RULES

Keep the rhythm. Protect the truth.

01

One dominant message per post.

02

Use real ASC3ND people and footage for documentary content. Do not manufacture human proof.

03

Monday explains. Wednesday connects. Friday activates.

04

Keep the black / cream visual rhythm disciplined and use gold sparingly.

05

Before publishing, verify names, dates, locations, claims, consent, and the intended next action.
